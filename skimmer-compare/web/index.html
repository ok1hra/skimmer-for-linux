What is compared

Comparisons in this range — one per decoder of the local skimmer

How the two skimmers are compared
  • Both telnet feeds are recorded with their receive time; only CQ spots are compared (the local skimmer spots nothing else), and only inside the frequency windows both receivers cover and while both feeds are up and the local receiver streams cleanly.
  • Each skimmer re-spots on its own schedule, so spots are merged into episodes (one call on one band) before they are paired: same call, overlapping in time and close in frequency is a match; similar calls at the same spot are a bust pair.
  • The referee for a callsign is the Reverse Beacon Network — another skimmer spotted it there (our own spots never count) — and MASTER.SCP when RBN has nothing.
  • Recall: share of real stations each skimmer found; the McNemar test says whether the stations found by one only are lopsided beyond chance. Bust rate: share of a skimmer's episodes with a wrong or unverifiable call. Speed: who spotted a matched station first.
  • A verdict needs p < 0.05 and enough stations; until then it says undecided.
