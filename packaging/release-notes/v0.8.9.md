The thermal guard now gives a paused band a second chance, and the Status page shows where each band goes: a heat map of the continents beside the bands. The decoder and the feed are as in v0.8.8.

## Thermal guard: a paused band is tested now and then

A band the guard paused gets no IQ, so it has no spots and could never show that its band had opened. On a machine that stays hot all day, the bands paused in the morning stayed off until the CPU cooled.

- **A band paused 30 minutes is tested:** it decodes for 5 minutes in place of the band with the fewest spots a minute, which is paused meanwhile, so the CPU load stays the same.
- **More spots a minute** than that band had (and 2 at least) — the two change places. Otherwise each goes back.
- One test at a time, the band waiting longest first. Pausing and bringing back wait during a test.
- Five minutes are enough: an open band spots in its first minute, and the band it would replace is the quietest one.
- **The band to pause** is now judged by spots a minute over the minutes it decoded, so a band that came back 5 minutes ago is not taken for the quietest.
- `[thermal] probe_every_min` (30; 0 = never) and `probe_min` (5), also in Settings → Thermal guard.
- The Status table marks the band on test with **TEST** and its spots so far; the guard's line says when each paused band is tested next.

## Status: where each band goes

- **A heat map of the continents** — EU, NA, SA, AS, AF, OC — replaces the *last spot* column. It shows how open each band is to each continent in the last 30 minutes, from the CW and RTTY spots and from WSPR.
- **How open, not how busy:** two loud stations from a continent mean the band is open (there is just no one else on), so a cell saturates fast instead of being scaled to Europe's dozens. Each station counts once, weighted by how loud it came.
- The colours are the map's heat map. Hover a cell for the number of stations, by mode, the loudest one and the calls.
- **The bands run from the highest on top to 160 m at the bottom.**
- **A band the guard paused** is on a dark grey ground along its row.

## Also new

- `/api/wspr` gives each station's newest decode per band (`seen`).

## Install

A skimmer on v0.8.8 with `[update] mode=auto` (the default) installs this release by itself at its update hour; Settings → Updates → *Install now* does it at once. By hand:

```sh
mv skimmer-headless-0.8.9-x86_64.AppImage skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
chmod +x skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
./skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
```
