The web page, ready for a public address: anyone can watch, only the machine itself manages. The waterfall plays the CW signal you click on and stays sharp when you zoom. The status, map and statistics pages are easier to read. The decoder and the feed are the same as in v0.8.2.

## A public page that stays yours

The page can be reachable from the internet. A visitor gets the status, the statistics and the waterfall. Settings and Log are for managers only. A request is a manager when all three hold:

- its address is in `[web] settings_from` (by default the machine itself);
- no proxy forwarded it;
- it is addressed to this machine by its IP or host name.

The rules, how to publish the page behind a router or a reverse proxy (nginx needs two extra lines), and what the audit fixed: [`docs/WEB-SECURITY.md`](https://github.com/ok1hra/skimmer-for-linux/blob/v0.8.3/docs/WEB-SECURITY.md).

- **DNS rebinding.** A web page in a browser on the skimmer's own machine could change the config and restart the skimmer. That is now refused.
- **A reverse proxy on the same machine** made every visitor a manager. Now everyone coming through a proxy is a viewer.
- **Visitors get only the public data.** Settings and Log are cut out of a visitor's page, and their APIs answer 403. `status.json` no longer shows the radio's or the peers' addresses, file paths or the `SKIM_*` switches.
- **Markup from a peer's feed** is no longer shown as part of the page: the spot's mode keeps letters and digits only, and calls, modes and countries are escaped.
- `stats.json` is cached for 20 s, and every reply carries a Content-Security-Policy and no-framing headers. At most 8 waterfall viewers and 4 listeners at a time.

## Waterfall

- **Listen.** A click on a signal plays it in the browser: a CW demodulator on the skimmer, passband 50–500 Hz (shown on the scale), a 600 Hz tone, 8 kB/s. The speaker button mutes it.
- **Sharp zoom.** The mouse wheel zooms smoothly, down to a 1 kHz span. While someone watches a band, the skimmer keeps 4 minutes of it at full FFT resolution, so after a zoom or a move the whole picture is drawn again sharp, not stretched. The spectrum follows the zoom at once.
- **The spotted calls** stand small at the spectrum's top edge for 15 minutes and are there as soon as the page opens.
- One **AUTO** button sets both the range and the floor. The spectrum is on by default, and the sliders are quieter.
- Leaving the tab for a moment stops nothing. After a longer absence the picture stays where it was.

## Status and statistics

- **Map.** Every call has a label: crowded labels move aside on a leader line. All layers are on by default, with quieter buttons.
- On a wide screen the **DX cluster** sits beside the map, and the line between them can be dragged. The locator warning gave way to an RBN link for your call. The band ladder's bars are in the band's colour.
- **Statistics.** CW and RTTY in pastels further apart, a calmer heatmap, no slider under the spots chart (the history's frame is the control). The CPU temperature in the header is coloured by its value.
- A **Git** item in the menu opens the project on GitHub.

## Before you upgrade

Nothing to do. The config, the statistics file and the feed are as in v0.8.2, and going back to v0.8.2 needs nothing either. To manage the skimmer from another machine, use an SSH tunnel (`ssh -L 8073:127.0.0.1:8073 <host>`, then `http://localhost:8073/`), or put that machine's address in `[web] settings_from`.
