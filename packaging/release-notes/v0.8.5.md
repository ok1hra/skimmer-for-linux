A round of fixes to the web page. The map sits on the station, the band list is easier to read, a restart no longer empties the page, and Settings warn about an unsaved change. The decoder and the feed are as in v0.8.4.

## Status page

- **Map centre.** Whoever may change the settings now gets the whole locator (JO60WA), and the map sits on the station. Before, it sat on the centre of the square, up to ~70 km away. The public page still gets the square only (JO60).
- **Bands beside the map.** They are listed by frequency, low band first, whatever order Settings has them in. An **eye** before each band hides it on the map only; the DX cluster keeps it, and the browser remembers the choice. The spots of the last half hour are shown as a **bar** in the band's colour, like the decoders. The counts are right as soon as the page opens; before, they showed 0 until the next refresh.
- **No RBN link on the status page any more.** The readouts beside the map still show who is connected to the RBN port and what went out.
- **A restart keeps the spots.** The map, the DX cluster and the band list keep the last 2 hours across a restart (Save, Restart, an upgrade). The spots are saved once a minute and at the end to `~/.local/state/skimmer-for-linux/spots.tsv`.

## Settings

- **Call and Locator** are written in capitals, whatever is typed. A call written small by hand in the config file is used in capitals too.
- **An unsaved change shows a bar in the header** on every tab, with Show, Save and restart, and Discard. The browser asks before the page is left with it, and Restart asks before it drops it. Going to another tab and back no longer reloads the form over the change.
- **RBN.** The section explains that the RBN takes spots only through the RBN Aggregator, connected to the skimmer's RBN port, and how to set it up. With the Aggregator on the same machine, do not use 7550: that is the Aggregator's own port for loggers. What an upload without the Aggregator would need: [`docs/RBN.md`](https://github.com/ok1hra/skimmer-for-linux/blob/v0.8.5/docs/RBN.md).

## Before you upgrade

Nothing to do. The config and the statistics file are as in v0.8.4, and going back to v0.8.4 needs nothing either (it ignores `spots.tsv`).
