CW decoder v3.1 — v3 with precision rules — and a new web page: an azimuthal map and a DX cluster on the status page, two years of statistics, several PCs behind one feed.

## Decoder: v3.1

v3.1 is v3 with three rules that stop its call reader from busting calls (design and the measurements: [`docs/CW-V3.md`](https://github.com/ok1hra/skimmer-for-linux/blob/v0.8.1/docs/CW-V3.md#precision-the-readers-own-busts-v31-the-default-since-v081)):

- a correction from the audio must beat the calls the text itself read, known to MASTER.SCP or not (no more W1KE "corrected" to W1KT);
- a correction the text does not support, or a short call the text did not read exactly, needs two separate reads;
- a call and its own longer reading on one frequency are one station that lost its tail: SM5D yields to SM5DAJ instead of MASTER.SCP keeping the full call off the feed.

Live against CW Skimmer Server on the same antenna (17 November, 7.5 hours): of 1020 stations either skimmer spotted, this one found 77.2 % and CW Skimmer Server 47.4 %; busted calls 4.6 % against 9.6 %.

The status reports the engine as `cw-v3.1`. `SKIM_V3_KR_RIVAL=0`, `SKIM_V3_KR_TWO=0` and `SKIM_GATE_HEAD=0` turn the rules off one by one; all three off is v0.8.0's v3. v2 and RTTY decode exactly as in v0.8.0.

## Web page

- **Status**: an azimuthal map of the last 30 minutes of spots around your locator (zoom fitted to the spots, grey line, state borders, DXCC prefixes; the cursor reads azimuth, distance and locator), and under it the DX cluster with a filter in every column — bands, the call as text or a regular expression, distance, bearing, dB, speed, mode, country — columns on and off, dB coloured by strength. Set `[station] locator` (Settings → Station and feed).
- **Statistics**: kept for two years (a minute for 7 days, 15 minutes for 90 days, an hour for 2 years), a time slider to go back, spots per hour with CW up and RTTY down, active decoders per band in both modes, CPU split into the CW and RTTY decoders with IQ lost on the same chart.
- **Settings**: export and import the config; a band can be switched off without deleting it.
- A new look, the same family as IP-rotator and wifilt; Roboto Condensed is built in, the page still needs no internet.

## Several PCs, one feed

- **Hub and workers**: a Red Pitaya streams to one client, so the PC that has it passes the IQ of the bands marked `@name` in its `[bands]` to a worker of that name (`[radio] type=hub` on the other PC), ~4.6 Mb/s per band at 96 kHz. The worker's spots go out on the hub's feed. [`docs/HUB.md`](https://github.com/ok1hra/skimmer-for-linux/blob/v0.8.1/docs/HUB.md).
- **Peers**: `[peers] hosts=` reads other skimmers' telnet feeds (also CW Skimmer Server) and sends their spots on under this call, duplicates dropped.
- Instances find each other on the LAN (`--find-skimmers`, Settings → Skimmers on the LAN).
- **RBN**: `[rbn]` opens a port for the RBN Aggregator with a delay of 0–99 s.

## Before you upgrade

- **The statistics file is converted** to a new format at the first start, its week merged into the longer rings. v0.8.0 cannot read the new file and would start its history afresh.
- **The hub port (7380) is open** on every instance by default, so a worker can find it; `[hub] port=0` closes it. `[hub] allow` limits who may connect (default: private networks).
- The statistics file is no longer rewritten every five minutes (about 1.2 GB a day of writes); only the new records are written in place (about 6 MB a day).
