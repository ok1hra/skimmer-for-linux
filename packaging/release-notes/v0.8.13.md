A web round on top of v0.8.12:
- the waterfall has an S-meter, and listening from another tab shows a live cut-out of the waterfall;
- **About** in the menu says who made the skimmer and whose decoders it runs on;
- **Bad calls** in Settings leaves calls out by prefix or whole call;
- the filters on the status page work together: the map, the bands table, its heat map and the DX cluster always show the same spots.

The decoders and the feed are as in v0.8.12.

## S-meter on the waterfall

- **What it shows:** the level in the audio's passband while you listen. It is drawn as an ICOM transceiver draws it: thin blocks along S1 … S9, then +20, +40, +60 dB in red. The level rises at once, falls 12 dB a second, and the peak is held for a second.
- **Where the level comes from:** the skimmer measures it before the audio's AGC, so a loud station reads loud.
- **Calibration:** the meter assumes 0 dBFS = +10 dBm (the Red Pitaya's ADC full scale, ±1 V into 50 Ω), with S9 = −73 dBm. Like a transceiver's meter, this is an estimate. Hover over the meter to see dBm and dBFS.

## Listening from another tab: a live cut-out

The pill in the corner (band, frequency, *mute*) now has a small waterfall over it:
- the frequency heard is in the middle, with 1 kHz either side;
- two lines mark the passband's edges.

Meanwhile the skimmer sends only this small window. Back on the Waterfall tab, the full picture is redrawn from the skimmer's last 4 minutes.

## Bad calls

- **Settings → Bad calls** (`[filter] bad=`) takes prefixes or whole calls, comma-separated, in any case:
  - a prefix drops every call that starts with it (`UA9` drops UA9ABC);
  - a whole call drops that call, also with a /part (`OK1ABC` drops OK1ABC/P and DL/OK1ABC).
- These calls are dropped where they are decoded, as with the callsign filter: no spot on the feed or to the RBN, and nothing on the page or in the statistics.
- **The callsign filter's preset regex is gone.** Ticking *Regex* on an empty field no longer fills in the expression that left out Russian and Belarusian calls, because it missed some. A config that already holds the expression keeps it; replace it with Bad calls if you like.

## One set of filters on the status page

- **A band's eye** in the bands table now hides the band everywhere: on the map, in the DX cluster and in the heat map. Before, it hid the band on the map only. It is the same switch as the DX cluster's band filter, so setting either one changes both.
- **The DX cluster's filters** (call, distance, bearing, dB, speed, country, source, age) now also decide what the bands table counts and what its heat map shows.
- **The DX cluster's columns** are in the order and alignment of [WIFILT](https://github.com/ok1hra/wifilt)'s DX cluster: UTC, kHz, DX, km (with an arrow turned to the bearing), From, Mode, dB and WPM, then Band and Country. The bearing's filter moved to the km column.
- **The map's mouse wheel** zooms only inside the map's circle. Outside it, the page scrolls.
- **A newly opened page starts on Status.** A reload, for example after *Save and restart*, stays on its tab.

## Install

A skimmer on v0.8.8 or later with `[update] mode=auto` (the default) installs this release by itself at its update hour. Settings → Updates → *Install now* does it at once. By hand:

```sh
mv skimmer-headless-0.8.13-x86_64.AppImage skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
chmod +x skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
./skimmer-headless-x86_64.AppImage
```
